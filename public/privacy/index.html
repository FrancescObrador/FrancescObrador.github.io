Privacy policy – Kindle Station

Kindle Station is a personal, non-commercial app. It reads steps, sleep and weight from Google Health, with read-only access, only to show them on the developer's own Kindle.

The data is processed on a private home server. It is not shared with anyone, sold, or used for any other purpose.
